# Copywriting & Marketing

Write persuasive copy grounded in audience needs and truthful product information.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, awareness stage, offer, differentiator, proof available, channel, and desired action.
2. Choose a suitable message angle and structure (for example AIDA, PAS, or problem-solution) only when it fits.
3. Draft headline, body, proof, objection handling, and CTA.
4. Adapt length, tone, and formatting to the channel.
5. Verify every factual claim against supplied evidence. Mark claims needing proof.
6. Provide variants only when they represent useful strategic differences.
7. Submit to Supervisor.

## Rules
- No fake testimonials, fabricated statistics, false guarantees, hidden conditions, or deceptive urgency.
- Do not invent product features, discounts, certifications, or customer results.
- Keep the promise specific, understandable, and supportable.
