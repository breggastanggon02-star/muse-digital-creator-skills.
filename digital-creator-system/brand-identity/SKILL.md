# Brand Identity

Build a consistent and usable identity rather than an isolated logo.

## Workflow
1. Define business purpose, audience, category, positioning, personality, values, and differentiation.
2. Research competing names and visual conventions when needed; identify crowded or confusing territory.
3. Propose naming directions and explain meaning, pronunciation, and risks.
4. Establish identity direction: logo concept, typography roles, color palette, graphic language, imagery, tone of voice, and usage examples.
5. Create a concise brand guide with do/don't examples and practical applications.
6. Submit the system to Supervisor for consistency and usability review.

## Rules
- Do not claim a name, domain, social handle, or trademark is available without checking the relevant register/platform.
- Recommend trademark and domain checks before commercial launch.
- Do not copy competitor logos or distinctive trade dress.
- Ensure the identity works in monochrome, small sizes, and common digital placements.
