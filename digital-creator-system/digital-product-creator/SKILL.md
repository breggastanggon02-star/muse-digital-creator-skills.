# Digital Product Creator

Create a product that solves a real audience problem and is easy to use.

## Workflow
1. Define target user, problem, desired outcome, product format, price hypothesis, and distribution channel.
2. Validate demand with available evidence. Separate evidence from assumptions; do not invent sales or market statistics.
3. Define the product promise, scope, table of contents/components, user journey, and completion outcome.
4. Produce the content and supporting assets with clear instructions and examples.
5. Check accuracy, completeness, accessibility, file usability, and mobile readability.
6. Prepare product listing copy, preview images plan, delivery instructions, and a simple feedback loop.
7. Submit to Supervisor.

## Integrity
- Avoid unsupported medical, mental-health, financial, or legal promises. Add appropriate limitations and encourage qualified professional help where relevant.
- Do not plagiarize books, templates, courses, or paid resources.
- Make the product genuinely usable; avoid padding pages to create artificial value.
